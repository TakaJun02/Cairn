# Dohara Falls

## Overview
Dohara Falls is a rare waterfall where underground spring water from Mount Chōkai gushes directly out of the rock surface. It has been selected as one of Yamagata Prefecture’s Top 100 Famous Waters. Rain and snowmelt from Mount Chōkai seep into the ground and eventually reemerge here as pristine spring water. The site is shrouded in a sacred and mysterious atmosphere.

## Highlights & Features
- **Twin Streams**: Water flows down on both sides of the small Fudō Hall at the center. According to local tradition, the taste of the water differs between the left and right sides.
- **Fresh Spring Water**: A water-drawing area is maintained for visitors. Many people come to collect the water for drinking, known for its constant cool temperature and refreshing taste throughout the year.
- **Mystical Ambience**: Surrounded by moss-covered rocks, cedar forests, and lush greenery, the area offers a cool and serene environment.
- **Fudō Hall**: Located at the center of the falls, this small shrine has long been known as a sacred training site.

## Basic Information
- **Location**: Yoshide, Yuza Town, Akumi District, Yamagata Prefecture
- **Opening Hours**: Open 24 hours
