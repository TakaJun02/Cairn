# Yunodai Onsen Chokaisanso

## Overview
Yunodai Onsen Chokaisanso is a hot spring inn located about 500 meters above sea level on the southern slope of Mt. Chokai. From the facility, you can enjoy sweeping views of the Shonai Plain and Mt. Gassan to the south and the majestic Mt. Chokai to the north. The lodge features large communal baths, open-air baths, private family baths, guest rooms, banquet halls, a restaurant, and a cozy lounge with a fireplace, making it an excellent base for hiking and sightseeing.

## Facility Information
- **Type**: Hot Spring Inn, Accommodation  
- **Features**:
    - **Hot Spring**: Sodium, calcium, and magnesium bicarbonate spring (low-tonicity, neutral, high-temperature).  
    - **Benefits**: Said to help with neuralgia, joint pain, muscle soreness, fatigue recovery, frozen shoulder, chronic digestive disorders, poor circulation, and chronic skin conditions.  
    - **Day-trip Bathing**: Available (11:00–21:00).  
    - **Bathing Fee**: Adults ¥520 / Children ¥170 (including bath tax).  
    - **Guest Rooms**: Approx. 22 rooms, both Japanese and Western styles.  
    - **Check-in / Check-out**: Check-in from 15:00 (latest 23:00) / Check-out by 10:00.  
    - **Parking**: Free parking for about 30 vehicles.  
    - **Shuttle Service**: Free pick-up and drop-off available to JR Sakata Station and Shoko Bus Terminal (reservation required in advance).  
    - **Banquet / Conference Facilities**: Large hall “Chokai,” training rooms, meeting rooms, and rest lounges.  
    - **Restaurant**: Main dining “Buna no Mori,” offering lunch 11:00–14:30 and dinner for overnight guests.  
    - **Shop & Lounge**: Local products, mountain plants, and a relaxing lounge with a fireplace.

## Access
- **By Train & Bus/Car**  
 About 30 minutes by car from JR Sakata Station (approx. 25 min by bus).  
 Alternatively, about 35 minutes by bus from Shoko Bus Terminal.  
- **By Car**  
 Approx. 35–40 minutes (22–23 km) from the Sakata-Minato IC on the Nihonkai-Tohoku Expressway.  
- **Shuttle Service**  
 Free pick-up and drop-off to JR Sakata Station or Shoko Bus Terminal (available 9:00–17:00, advance reservation required).  
- **Note**  
 Reservations are recommended for the shuttle service due to passenger limits and cut-off times.

## Day-trip Bathing & Banquet Plans
- **Hours**: 11:00–21:00  
- **Closed Days**: Open year-round (occasional temporary closures)  
- **Fees**: Adults ¥520 / Children ¥170 (including bath tax)  
- **Private Bath**: Free private bath “Amanogawa” available for 60 minutes on a first-come-first-served basis.  
- **Day-trip Banquet Plans**: Include bathing, rest, and meals; from around ¥4,500 per person (reservation required, free shuttle for groups of 10 or more).  

## Hiking & Sightseeing
- Serves as a convenient base for Mt. Chokai hiking routes on the southern side.  
- Some lodging plans offer local guided walks such as morning nature tours.  

## Notes
- The open-air bath is typically closed from January to April due to snow.  
- Restaurant lunch hours are 11:00–14:30; dinner service is available for staying guests.  
- For banquet, shuttle, and day-trip bath usage, advance inquiry and booking are strongly recommended.  
- Facility information and prices are subject to change; please check the official website or contact the lodge for the latest details.
