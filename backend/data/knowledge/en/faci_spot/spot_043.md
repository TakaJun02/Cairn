# Hanadate Clean Heights

## Overview
Hanadate Clean Heights is a tourism hub located in the Chōkai Highlands of Yurihonjo City, Akita Prefecture. It offers a souvenir shop, a tourist information corner, and a restaurant, serving as a convenient stop for visitors exploring the Chōkai Highlands.

## Highlights & Features
- **Souvenir Shop**: A variety of local specialties and souvenirs are available for purchase.
- **Restaurant**: The restaurant is popular for its rich Jersey soft-serve ice cream. Other hearty menu options include fried chicken set meals and spicy miso ramen with green onions.
- **Tourist Information**: A dedicated information corner provides useful guidance and travel tips about the local area.

## Basic Information
- **Location**: 96 Hanadate, Jonai, Yashima-machi, Yurihonjo City, Akita Prefecture
- **Opening Hours**: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Closed on Wednesdays)
- **Closed**: Wednesdays

## Access
- **By Car**: About 1 hour from Honjo IC on the Nihonkai-Tohoku Expressway.
- **By Train**: About 20 minutes by car from JR Yashima Station.
