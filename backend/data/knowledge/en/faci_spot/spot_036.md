# Chokai Kogen Hanadate Bokujo Koen

## Overview
Located at the foot of Mount Chōkai, this spacious highland area is home to a ranch and leisure facilities. Visitors can interact with Jersey cows and other animals, taste fresh dairy products, and enjoy a variety of outdoor activities.

## Highlights & Features
- **Jersey Cows & Dairy Products**: Fresh milk from Jersey cows is processed into soft-serve ice cream, cheese, and butter, available at the on-site dairy workshop “Milgee.”
- **Dairy Gourmet**: At the attached “Jersey House Café,” you can savor special treats such as soft-serve ice cream made from rich Jersey milk.
- **Variety of Leisure Facilities**: The park offers park golf, go-karts, pony riding, and more, making it enjoyable for both children and adults.
- **Accommodation Options**: Log cabins and camping sites are available, allowing visitors to fully enjoy the natural surroundings.

## Basic Information
- **Location**: Hanadate, Jonai, Yashima-machi, Yurihonjo City, Akita Prefecture
- **Operating Season**: Varies by facility. Some facilities are open 24 hours or only on weekends according to Google Maps; please check in advance.
- **Fees**: Vary by facility. Entrance is generally free, but activities and certain facilities may require a fee.

## Access
- **By Car**: About 45 minutes from Honjo IC on the Nihonkai-Tohoku Expressway.
