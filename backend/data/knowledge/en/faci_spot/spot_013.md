# Chokai Marimo

## Overview
Chokai Marimo refers to rare spherical moss formations found exclusively in the Shishigahana Wetland within the Nakajimadai Recreational Forest at the northwestern foot of Mt. Chokai, Nikaho City, Akita Prefecture. These unique green spheres, formed mainly by moss species such as *Hylocomium splendens*, gradually intertwine and grow into ball-shaped colonies over decades. Some can reach diameters of over one meter. This phenomenon is extremely rare, with only a few confirmed locations in Japan and worldwide, making it of high scientific value.

## Highlights & Features
- **Mysterious Green Spheres**: The moss balls floating or resting in cold, clear spring water create a magical, otherworldly scene.
- **Globally Rare Habitat**: Chokai Marimo is among the few known sites in Japan and one of the rarest moss ball habitats globally.
- **Pristine Wetland Setting**: Surrounded by ancient beech forest and cool acidic spring water, the wetland offers a serene and mystical atmosphere.

## Basic Information
- **Location**: Shishigahana Wetland, Nakajimadai Recreational Forest, Yokooka, Kisakata-machi, Nikaho City, Akita Prefecture
- **Hours**: Daytime (approx. May to November)
- **Fee**: Free

## Access
- **By Car**: About 15 minutes from the Kisakata IC on the Nihonkai-Tohoku Expressway. Parking is available at Nakajimadai Recreational Forest.
- **On Foot**: From the parking lot, follow the wooden paths through the wetland for about 30–40 minutes to reach the Chokai Marimo viewing area.
- **By Public Transport**: About 20 minutes by taxi from JR Kisakata Station.
